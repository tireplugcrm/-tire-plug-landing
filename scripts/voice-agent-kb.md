## New tires
- Prices are per tire. Mount and balance is a flat $20 a wheel for phone callers, every size up to 23 inches (the website shows $25). We don't mount 24-inch wheels and up (those tires are carry-out only).
- Tire prices are quoted PER TIRE, tire only (check_tires gives it). Mount and balance is $20 a wheel on the phone, mentioned only when they ask: a $78 tire is $98 a tire installed. $20 is the lowest; there's no further install discount. Tax (10% on the tires) is added at the shop and mentioned only if asked; never quote a total with tax. There's no separate tire disposal fee.
- Valve stems: $5 for all four, or $2 each (add-on).
- Special orders: we can get brands like Toyo and Michelin that aren't on our shelf or next-day list. They're priced from a supplier portal, so take the size and save_lead ("special order: Toyo") and say the team will text the price, or book them in.
- Tires on the shelf can go on the same day. Tires we order in (next-day) are installed tomorrow or later, never the same day.
- Budget tires last about 40,000 to 50,000 miles; mid-range (like Lexani) about 70,000; premium brands include Michelin, Goodyear, Continental, Bridgestone and Falken.
- Brands we feature: Kumho, RBP, Landspider, Lexani, Evoluxx, Blackhawk and more. Always check stock with check_tires; never promise a brand is in stock without it.
- Remington 155/80R13 whitewalls for lowriders and classics.
- Don't know your size? Give year, make and model, or text a sidewall photo to 562-500-4625.
- Tires usually last 3 to 6 years; replace at 2/32" tread or at 6 years old.

## Brakes (pads)
- How the shop prices brake pads: the parts price for that exact car plus $60 per wheel, so $120 per axle. Cars with an electric parking brake are $75 more (it's a harder job).
- Brake prices are quoted before tax (no tax total for brakes).
- Brake pads start at about $150 an axle (front or rear), before tax. All four wheels usually run about $300. Example: a 2012 Toyota Camry is about $155 front, $151 rear, about $306 for all four with standard pads.
- Brand: we use the best-value pads AutoZone has for the car (usually Duralast, with a lifetime warranty); check_brakes names the brand and warranty for each car. Rotors come with a manufacturer's warranty.
- Exact brake price: check_brakes has the exact parts price for many cars (AutoZone's cheapest pads and rotors + our labor). Use it whenever you have the year, make and model.
- To spot an electric parking brake, ask: "Does your car have a parking brake button instead of a lever or pedal?" A button means electric: add $75 for the rear brakes.
- The exact price depends on the car's parts, so take year, make and model (a VIN or license plate works too) and the team confirms it, or book the visit.
- Rotors aren't included in the pad price. Rotors are priced the same way: the rotor's parts price plus $40 each, usually about $95 a rotor, so about $190 more per axle. Example: 2012 Camry front pads and rotors together are about $348. Grinding usually means the rotors need replacing; the tech confirms it free at the visit.

## Why an alignment with new tires
- The one-sentence pitch: "An alignment keeps your new tires wearing evenly so they last longer, and since you're getting all four it's $10 off."
- New tires wear evenly and last longer when the car is aligned; a bad alignment can eat a new set early. Signs you need one: car pulls to one side, crooked steering wheel, uneven wear.

## Warranty and repairs
- Every tire has the manufacturer's warranty: it covers defects in how the tire was made, not normal wear or road damage.
- Free flat repairs for the first year (365 days) on repairable punctures.
- If a tire can't be repaired (sidewall damage, a big cut, a blowout), the free repair and the manufacturer's warranty don't cover it. The Road Hazard Warranty does: an optional one-time add-on at purchase that replaces the tire. The shop confirms its price at the visit.
- Each tire's treadwear (mileage) warranty comes from check_tires (its "warranty" field), for example Falken Ziex ZE960 A/S: 65,000 miles on H/V-rated sizes.
- Falken tires come with a 2-year road hazard warranty from Falken, on top of the treadwear warranty. It's a selling point: say it when quoting Falken.
- RBP Repulsor A/T: 2-year road hazard (or until 3/32" tread), next-day exchange, 50,000-mile treadwear warranty.
- Kumho: up to 75,000-mile treadwear warranty (depends on the tire), road hazard replacement in the first 12 months, 30-day satisfaction guarantee.
